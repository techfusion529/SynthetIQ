# SynthetIQ
**Autonomous Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Compliance & Anti-Fraud Engine**

## 1. The Use Case & Problem Statement
In the circular economy, Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) mandates that corporations (Producers, Importers, and Brand Owners — PIBOs) offset their plastic footprint by purchasing recycling certificates from Plastic Waste Processors (PWPs). 

**The Real-World Crisis:** The current ecosystem relies on blind trust, manual PDF MOUs, and disjointed portals. This has led to the "Ghost Certificate" crisis, where fraudulent recyclers generate digital compliance credits without melting a single ton of physical plastic. For enterprise PIBOs, this creates massive financial exposure and retroactive legal liability when regulatory bodies (like India's CPCB) invalidate fake certificates. 

**The SynthetIQ Solution:** SynthetIQ bridges the gap between cloud-based AI and industrial process automation. It is a zero-trust, multi-agent platform that completely automates EPR liability calculation, market procurement, and statutory dispatch. Crucially, it replaces manual trust with a **Quad-Core Fraud Audit**, utilizing machine-level SCADA telemetry and System 1 AI to mathematically prove thermodynamic melting occurred before any financial settlement clears.

## 2. Multi-Workflow Architecture & Agent Ecosystem

SynthetIQ is not a monolithic script; it is composed of four decoupled, highly resilient workflows orchestrated by Temporal, ensuring process automation that survives API crashes and spot-node preemptions.

### Workflow 1: Upstream Liability & Sourcing Planning
Calculates exact physical and digital compliance debt by continuously monitoring corporate ERP data against fluctuating government rules.
*   **Brand Liability Agent (Gemini 3.8 Flash):** Queries BigQuery ERP sales data to calculate physical recycled content mandates and current-year digital deficits, factoring in historic debt amortization.
*   **Regulatory Watchdog Agent (Gemini 3.8 Flash):** Parses unstructured government PDFs into strict JSON rulesets (e.g., Conversion Factors, Consent to Operate limits) and routes the mandate based on chemistry (Mechanical vs. Co-processing).

### Workflow 2: Liquidity & Dual-Mode Auction
Secures the best market price for the mandated volume within strict regulatory boundaries.
*   **Treasury Agent (Gemini 3.8 Flash):** Broadcasts an RFP and executes a continuous double auction, strictly locked between the 30% and 100% statutory compensation corridor.
*   **Recycler Bidder Agents (Heuristics):** Simulated market participants bidding based on their active plant inventory and verified government capacity.

### Workflow 3: Quad-Core Fraud Audit (The Core Reflex)
Proves mathematically and physically that the waste was transported and thermodynamically melted, defeating IoT spoofing.
*   **Logistics Agent (Gemini 3.8 Flash):** Verifies inbound E-Way bills and scans the national QR-code packaging ledger to establish material origin.
*   **Auditor Agent (TypeSafe Jev):** The high-speed System 1 reflex layer. It bypasses basic smart meters, extracting deep SCADA/VFD torque and power factor arrays via the MCP Server. Jev instantly evaluates this electrical signature to detect spoofed resistance-heater loads, returning a calibrated confidence score on physical melt verification.

### Workflow 4: Settlement & Statutory Dispatch
Manages the corporate financial payout securely while generating cryptographically signed government compliance forms.
*   **ERP Agent (Deterministic/Jev):** Upon human approval via the secure dashboard, this agent creates an 80/20 Split-Payment Escrow Purchase Order in SAP/Oracle, protecting the brand from retroactive registration revocations.
*   **Legal Agent / Form Serializer (Pydantic Models):** Applies the strict $C_f$ Conversion Factor, generates the Form-1 JSON, applies the Digital Signature (DSC), and dispatches to the government API using Temporal's exponential backoff to survive portal downtime.

## 3. Technology Stack

| Layer | Technology | Purpose in SynthetIQ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Orchestration** | Temporal (on GKE Spot Instances) | Durable execution of the 4 sub-workflows, ensuring state preservation and retry logic for external API failures. |
| **System 2 AI (Reasoning)** | Gemini 3.8 Flash | Complex strategy: auction evaluation, regulatory parsing, and structuring dynamic SQL queries. |
| **System 1 AI (Reflexes)** | TypeSafe AI (Jev) | High-speed, zero-hallucination primitive routing (`Noul`, `Score`) for the SCADA anti-fraud audit. |
| **Data Privacy** | Gemma 2B (Local Ollama Pod) | In-cluster scrubbing of PII and proprietary corporate SKUs before data reaches external LLMs. |
| **Zero-Trust Boundary** | Model Context Protocol (MCP) | Securely isolates credentials. Agents must call MCP tools to access BigQuery, Pub/Sub, and external APIs. |
| **Data & Telemetry** | Google BigQuery & Pub/Sub | BigQuery acts as the ERP ledger; Pub/Sub streams the simulated industrial SCADA/VFD telemetry. |
| **Edge / Presentation** | Firebase (Hosting & Auth) | Zero-cost, CDN-backed global hosting for the Executive Dashboard, utilizing role-based JWTs for the Human-in-the-Loop approval gate. |
| **API Gateway** | Cloud Run (FastAPI) | Serverless webhook receiver that validates Firebase tokens and signals the internal Temporal cluster. |

### C. Business Impact & Feasibility
SynthetIQ addresses a multi-million-dollar regulatory risk for Fortune 500 companies. By mirroring the exact CPCB (Central Pollution Control Board) 2026 mandates—including the 1/3rd amortization rule, dual-mode recycled content sourcing, and the $C_f$ conversion factor—the platform proves it is not a theoretical toy, but a commercially viable Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) extension.
